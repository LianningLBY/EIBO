Dear Editors,

We submit “Finite-sample certificates for the Bayes accuracy gain of feature fusion in QUIC traffic” for consideration in Information Fusion. We develop lower confidence bounds on the improvement in Bayes accuracy from adding a feature to an existing representation. The method allows arbitrary dependence within each time block and assumes conditional independence across blocks.

We compare four bounds on the same held-out flows and fitted rules in two encrypted traffic corpora. For the 44-class CESNET-QUICEXT-25 XS cohort, the pooled bound on the benefit of adding packet timing is 2.03 percentage points in the original evaluation window and 1.94 points in a disjoint window under the daily block model. We also report how the pointwise bounds change with calendar alignment at wider block sizes.

The study fits Information Fusion’s scope in feature fusion and network security. The method decomposes the gain into nonnegative contributions that can be bounded simultaneously. A controlled simulation shows how the concentration of gain and the number of occupied cells affect the choice between pooling and separate cell bounds. The result helps readers assess whether a new observation is informative enough to establish a gain under a stated sampling model.
