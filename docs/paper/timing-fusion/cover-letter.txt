We submit “Finite-sample certificates for Bayes accuracy gains from feature fusion under block dependence” for consideration in Information Fusion. The paper develops lower confidence bounds on the improvement in Bayes accuracy from adding a feature to an existing representation. The method allows arbitrary dependence within each evaluation block and assumes conditional independence across blocks.

The evaluation compares four bounds on two encrypted traffic corpora and the KU-HAR activity dataset. For the 44-class CESNET-QUICEXT-25 XS cohort, adding packet timing yields a pooled daily bound of 2.035 percentage points in the original window and 1.935 in a retrospective check of an earlier, disjoint window. On KU-HAR, adding gyroscope measurements to the accelerometer representation gives a 2.419-point lower bound with 59 held-out participants as blocks. An exact zero-gain experiment quantifies coverage under two forms of cross-block dependence.

The work addresses the evaluation of nested feature fusion. The method decomposes the gain into nonnegative contributions that can be bounded simultaneously. A controlled simulation shows how the concentration of gain and the number of occupied cells affect the choice between pooling and separate cell bounds.
