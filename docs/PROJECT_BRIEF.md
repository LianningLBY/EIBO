## Summary
Open-set encrypted-traffic recognition is empirical (SepSpace, RoNeTC report AUROC/OSCR with no stated limits), while the relevant theory sits in two disconnected pieces: Cherubin (PETS 2017) gives feature-set-conditional Bayes-error lower bounds for closed-set website fingerprinting, and the Liu–Cheng–Liu line proves side-channel leakage inevitability — arXiv 2602.14055's existence theorem is single-letter (one observation window, one feature) with exponential error decay only under an explicit i.i.d. repeated-observation assumption (its §5.1), and its rate-distortion follow-up (arXiv 2607.17889) derives the leakage–cost Pareto frontier strictly within the stationary-memoryless class Theta_iid (this restriction is abstract-verified; whether the paper additionally poses the multi-letter case as an open problem is a pre-writing full-text verification task and is not load-bearing). This project occupies that verified gap, with a LEAD contribution and a FRAMEWORK contribution explicitly ordered. Lead: a measurement-grounded estimation of epsilon-indistinguishability BRACKETS for open-set QUIC service recognition — for each service pair, a two-sided bracket [epsilon_k^lo, epsilon_k^hi] on the total-variation distance between joint k-packet-prefix laws on a protocol-quotiented observable alphabet, with explicitly graded certification: epsilon_k^lo is an assumption-free classifier-based lower bound that certifies distinguishability (capping any error floor from above), while epsilon_k^hi — the side an error-floor claim actually needs — is distribution-free only in the small-k discrete regime (plug-in concentration on the finite quotient alphabet), where alone the term 'certified floor' error >= (1-epsilon_k^hi)/2 is used; larger-k floors are labeled model-conditional. This directionality discipline, together with a paired-sampling analysis of whether content semantics separate from environment factors (client implementation, country, collection month) on CESNET-QUICEXT-25's 12 months / 194.3M flows (DST_ASN demoted from environment axis to audited confound), is the paper's empirical core. Framework: the multi-letter theory — Le Cam/Bretagnolle–Huber bounds driven by the chain-rule joint divergence over the dependent packet prefix (predicted sublinear growth in k against the linear i.i.d. null, adjudicated in a pre-registered moderate-k window k in [5,15] where two-sided brackets exist), an admissible alphabet derived from RFC 9000/9312 and empirically upgraded to implementation-conditioned constraints via QUIC_CLIENT_VERSION groups, exact spec-forced equivalence classes as epsilon=0 corollaries, and a deployment-site missing-service-mass term estimated by Good-Toulmin with prediction intervals — scoped as a site-specific quantity, not a universal unknown prior. Validation is pre-registered one-sided coverage on an informative-pair stratum (epsilon_hat_k^hi < 0.5) with a binomial violation test, so trivial coverage on well-separated pairs cannot dilute the check. Evaluation keeps the maximal-hygiene regime: flow- and time-disjoint splits, packet-level splitting prohibited (SIGCOMM'25), mandated address-field randomization (SHORTCUTCATCHER, CoNEXT 2026), and an SNI/ECH sensitivity analysis reporting the bound delta between SNI-inclusive and SNI-excluded channels as a measured price of ECH. Primary timeline explicitly assumes the human-in-loop execution mode (2.5–3.5 months), which is what makes USENIX Security 2027 Cycle 2 (paper deadline 2027-01-26) reachable; the manual-mode estimate (7–9 months) targets the fallback venues instead.

## Core Problem
Two coupled questions with an explicit priority order. Q1-LEAD (measured limits): Given the QUIC wire image and a bounded observation window (first k packets: direction, size, timing), what is the measured irreducible open-set error for rejecting an unknown service against a known label set with zero unknown-class calibration samples — expressed as a QUANTITATIVE epsilon-indistinguishability BRACKET: for each pair, a two-sided interval [epsilon_k^lo, epsilon_k^hi] on epsilon_k = TV(P_1^(1:k), P_2^(1:k)) over the admissible quotient alphabet, with the two sides carrying explicitly different certification strengths. The lower side (classifier-based, assumption-free) certifies distinguishability and caps any error floor from above; the upper side — which the floor claim error >= (1-epsilon_k)/2 (Le Cam) actually requires — is distribution-free only in the small-k discrete regime via plug-in concentration on the finite quotient alphabet, and model-conditional beyond it. 'Certified floor' is reserved for the former regime; the absence of a good classifier is never treated as evidence of indistinguishability. The Bretagnolle–Huber complement error >= (1/4)exp(-D_k) uses the chain-rule joint KL divergence over the dependent prefix. The alphabet A_k is derived from RFC 9000/9312 (client Initial padding to >= 1200 bytes censors early size marginals; coalescing quotients datagram sizes; header protection and missing 1-RTT length fields restrict observable framing) and then VERIFIED per client implementation via QUIC_CLIENT_VERSION grouping, because implementations demonstrably deviate from spec floors — the theory is conditioned on measured constraints, not the paper spec. Exact spec-forced indistinguishability classes survive as epsilon=0 corollaries. The unknown-class mass entering the open-set bound is scoped as a deployment-site missing service mass, Good-Toulmin-estimated from frequency-of-frequencies over fine-grained host identifiers with prediction intervals and a 1:100-sampling sensitivity analysis — never a universal unknown prior. The theory-discriminating prediction: D_k grows sublinearly in k under within-flow dependence, whereas the verified i.i.d. assumption in arXiv 2602.14055 §5.1 predicts linear growth; adjudication is PRE-REGISTERED to k in [5,15], the window where two-sided brackets exist — at larger k only the assumption-free lower bracket survives, so growth-rate evidence there is structurally weakest and is demoted to exploratory. Q2 (separability): are content semantics and environment factors identifiably separable in this channel? Formalize the observable law P_{s,e} with e = (client version, destination country, collection month) — DST_ASN excluded because service and AS are near-coreferential for self-hosted services; it is audited and ablated instead. Separability = existence of a statistic T that is e-invariant within services while s-discriminative uniformly over e; identifiability requires services crossed with environment values, verified by the Step-0 go/no-go cell-occupancy gate before any sweep. Falsifiers: the held-out-environment generalization gap against the gap predicted by environment-conditional divergences, plus an environment-probing classifier. Validation of Q1 against deployed detectors is by pre-registered one-sided coverage restricted to informative pairs (epsilon_hat_k^hi < 0.5) with a binomial violation-count test — a falsifiable containment that neither rank correlation nor trivial coverage on well-separated pairs can fake.

## Opportunity
No existing work connects information-theoretic indistinguishability limits to deployed open-set traffic detectors with certification-graded, falsifiable brackets. The pieces all exist separately — classifier-based TV lower bounds (arXiv 2005.06006), discriminative TV estimation (arXiv 2405.15337), single-letter leakage-inevitability theory (arXiv 2602.14055 / 2607.17889, confined to Theta_iid), hygiene-audited QUIC datasets with client-version and 12-month coverage (CESNET-QUICEXT-25) — and the assembly is the contribution: measured epsilon-brackets with honest directionality, a dependent-prefix chain-rule theory testable against the i.i.d. null in a pre-registered k window, and an environment-content separability protocol that the dataset's paired structure makes identifiable for the first time.

## Approach
Step 0 — Go/no-go feasibility gate (unchanged). On the QUICEXT-25 XS subset plus one full month, audit environment-cell occupancy for e = (QUIC_CLIENT_VERSION, DST_COUNTRY, month of TIME_FIRST): count services observed in >= 2 client-version values, >= 2 months, and (where applicable) >= 2 countries. GATE: proceed with the full paired design only if >= 15 of the 50 services survive full crossing; otherwise fall back to the (client version x month) two-axis design, which the 12-month span guarantees is populated. In the same pass, run the ASN-confound audit: per-service entropy of DST_ASN and per-ASN entropy of service labels, flagging single-AS services (Google-family etc.) where ASN and service are near-coreferential. DST_ASN is EXCLUDED from the environment axes by design — it enters only this audit and the address-randomization ablation. Step 1 — Spec-derived admissible alphabet A_k from RFC 9000/9312, as before: (a) client Initial datagrams padded to >= 1200 bytes and expanded server ack-eliciting Initials censor the packet-1/2 size marginals; (b) coalescing makes the wire-observable datagram size a sum over coalesced long-header packets, so the observable channel is the pushforward of the packet process under this quotient map and all divergences are computed on the quotient; (c) 1-RTT short-header packets carry no length field and header protection hides packet numbers. Exact spec-forced equivalence classes are stated as corollaries (the epsilon = 0 stratum), not as the headline theorem. Step 2 — Implementation verification (unchanged): group flows by QUIC_CLIENT_VERSION and empirically test whether each client implementation satisfies — or exceeds — the spec-derived constraints: location and spread of the packet-1 size point mass (1200 is a floor, not a constant), coalescing patterns, second-flight structure. Cross-check against documented per-implementation Initial-packet differences (tlsfingerprint.io QUIC campaign; uQUIC's implementation-specific Initial mimicry; Smith et al. PETS 2021). Output: an implementation-conditioned alphabet A_k^impl per client group with measured deviation tables. Step 3 — Quantitative epsilon-indistinguishability theory (core). For any service pair, minimax open-set error >= (1 - epsilon_k)/2 where epsilon_k = TV(P_1^(1:k), P_2^(1:k)) on the quotient alphabet (Le Cam), complemented by the Bretagnolle–Huber form error >= (1/4)exp(-D_k) with D_k the CHAIN-RULE joint KL divergence — never an i.i.d. tensorization. POSITIONING DISCIPLINE (revised per review): the claims written into the positioning section are limited to what is abstract-verified — arXiv 2602.14055's main theorem is single-letter and its Section 5.1 exponential decay assumes i.i.d. repeated observations; arXiv 2607.17889's rate-distortion Pareto frontier is proved strictly within the stationary-memoryless class Theta_iid (verified from its abstract, including the exact-frontier-within-Theta_iid statement). The stronger sentence 'the multi-letter/adaptive formulation is stated as an open problem' is NOT abstract-visible and is flagged as a pre-writing full-text verification task; the project's gap claim does not depend on that sentence, only on the verified Theta_iid restriction. Predicted D_k growth is sublinear in k under within-flow dependence; linear growth is the i.i.d. null. M-class extension via Fano with Varshamov–Gilbert pruning; prescribed-error variant keeps false-accept/false-reject asymmetric under the zero-unknown-prior constraint. Good-Toulmin missing-mass term scoped as deployment-site missing service mass, estimated from the flow-level frequency-of-frequencies over fine-grained anonymized destination-host identifiers, with Eriksson-type prediction intervals and mandatory 1:100-sampling sensitivity analysis; bounds reported with and without this term. Step 4 — Environment-content separability protocol on axes e = (QUIC_CLIENT_VERSION, DST_COUNTRY, month): estimate (i) within-service cross-environment divergence vs (ii) cross-service within-environment divergence on the k-packet observable; separability iff (i) << (ii) uniformly. Two falsifiers: the environment-probing classifier (predict e from observables within a fixed service) and the held-out-environment generalization gap (train on E_train, test on disjoint E_test) against the gap predicted by the estimated environment-conditional divergence. Step 5 — TWO-SIDED BRACKET ESTIMATION with explicit certification strengths (RESTRUCTURED per review — this fixes the directionality gap). Every pair's epsilon_k is reported as a bracket [epsilon_k^lo, epsilon_k^hi] whose two sides have different epistemic status, stated explicitly in every table and claim: (LOWER SIDE, assumption-free) any calibrated discriminator's balanced accuracy a yields TV >= 2a - 1; we use the high-probability classifier-based TV lower bounds of Michel et al. (arXiv 2005.06006, sup-over-cutoff in-class-accuracy form) so epsilon_k^lo is a certified, distribution-free LOWER bound on epsilon_k. Directionality made explicit: epsilon_k^lo certifies DISTINGUISHABILITY — it caps the achievable-error floor from ABOVE ((1-epsilon_k)/2 <= (1-epsilon_k^lo)/2) and can never by itself certify an error floor. (UPPER SIDE, regime-dependent) epsilon_k^hi is what the floor claim needs, and it is certified only where it can be: (a) SMALL-k DISCRETE REGIME — on the low-cardinality early-prefix quotient alphabet, the plug-in TV estimator is upward-biased in expectation and concentrates (McDiarmid / Bretagnolle–Huber–Carol over the finite alphabet), giving a distribution-free upper confidence bound on epsilon_k from the millions of available flows; ONLY in this regime is the term 'certified floor' used, meaning error >= (1 - epsilon_k^hi)/2 with finite-sample confidence and no model assumptions; (b) LARGER-k REGIME — epsilon_k^hi comes from cross-fitted per-service autoregressive density models (LightGBM over PPI triplets or a small AR sequence model, chain-rule decomposition term-by-term) plus a reported model-misspecification diagnostic; floors derived here are labeled MODEL-CONDITIONAL FLOORS in all text, tables, and the abstract — never 'certified'. The discriminative TV estimator of Nguyen et al. (arXiv 2405.15337) serves as the third triangulation point interpolating the two regimes. Failure to train a good classifier is explicitly NOT treated as evidence that epsilon_k is small. Prespecified agreement criterion between the three estimators gates every downstream k-threshold claim; where they disagree at some k, only the assumption-free sides (epsilon_k^lo everywhere, epsilon_k^hi in the discrete regime) are reported. SPLIT POLICY unchanged and mandatory: flow-disjoint AND time-disjoint (train June 2024–January 2025, test February–May 2025; QUIC22 four-week replication); packet-level splitting prohibited (SIGCOMM'25 leakage findings). Step 6 — Detector validation with PRE-REGISTERED coverage design (revised per review). Known/unknown splits holding out 5/10/20% of QUICEXT-25's 50 labels (and QUIC22's 102); detectors: LightGBM on k-packet features, SepSpace-style prototype-radius rejector, RoNeTC-style uncertainty-quantified rejector, frozen Pcap-Encoder probe; metrics OSCR and FPR95 per held-out service. Primary validation is one-sided coverage — every observed per-pair detector error must respect the predicted floor (1 - epsilon_hat_k^hi)/2 within its CI — but with a PRE-REGISTERED INFORMATIVE-PAIR CRITERION to prevent trivial-coverage dilution: only pairs with epsilon_hat_k^hi < 0.5 (predicted floor > 0.25, the reviewer-suggested threshold) enter the primary coverage statistic; pairs with 0.5 <= epsilon_hat_k^hi < 0.9 form a secondary stratum; pairs above 0.9 are reported but excluded from validation claims. PRE-REGISTERED TEST STATISTIC: the number of floor violations among informative pairs, compared against a one-sided binomial test at the nominal CI coverage level; any excess violation rate falsifies either the estimate or the channel model and must be reported. If the informative stratum is empty at some k (watchpoint 1), that k contributes no validation evidence and this is stated, not papered over. Spearman rank-ordering of per-service false-accept rates by estimated divergence is secondary evidence. The functional-form test (sublinear vs linear D_k growth) is PRE-REGISTERED to the moderate-k window k in [5, 15] — the regime where two-sided brackets exist (per review: at large k only the one-sided lower bracket survives, so large-k growth claims would rest on the weakest evidence exactly where discrimination is needed); k > 15 growth curves are reported as exploratory only. Step 7 — Ablations designed to disprove the mechanism (unchanged): (i) mandated address-field randomization (DST_IP, DST_IP_SUBNET, DST_ASN, DST_COUNTRY) at test time for any baseline consuming them, before/after delta reported (SHORTCUTCATCHER, CoNEXT 2026); our channel excludes these by construction so its predicted bound must be invariant — itself a check; (ii) test-only feature shuffles (sizes, timings) with predicted divergence direction; (iii) near-coincident-pair check: detector error must track the measured epsilon(k) bracket, and separation onset must match the chain-rule (not i.i.d.) k-threshold within the pre-registered k window; (iv) environment-conditioned re-estimation whenever the environment-probing classifier beats chance, reporting how much apparent service signal was environment; (v) SNI/ECH sensitivity analysis: the anonymized destination-host identifier is a deterministic function of the SNI, so a classifier given it upper-bound-emulates a plaintext-SNI attacker; report the bound and detector deltas between SNI-inclusive and SNI-excluded channels as a measured 'price of ECH'.
